Questions

Common questions

How much does window cleaning cost?

It depends on the home: how many windows there are, how many stories, and how easy the glass is to reach. Hard water removal, pressure washing and gutter cleaning are priced by the size of the job and how much buildup there is. Our pricing and cost guide explains the factors and the typical market ranges for this area. The estimate itself is free, and you get a clear price before any work starts.

What services do you offer?

Four: window cleaning inside and out, hard water removal for the mineral spots that build up on glass, pressure washing for driveways, walkways and patios, and gutter cleaning. The services list above explains what each one includes.

Do you clean windows in my town?

We work in Everett, Snohomish, Seattle and Bellevue, WA, and everywhere in between. See where we work for the details, or call or text 509-770-5633 if you are not sure whether we reach you.

Can you do the windows, gutters and driveway in one visit?

Yes. You can book window cleaning, gutter cleaning and pressure washing together so it all gets done in one trip. Each job is priced on its own factors in the same free estimate. Just say what you want included when you reach out.

How do I get an exact price?

Call or text 509-770-5633, or send the estimate form on this page. It helps to share your address, what needs cleaning, and roughly how many windows and stories your home has. You get a clear price back before any work starts, and the estimate is free.

Are you licensed?

Yes. One Way Window Cleaning LLC is licensed, and estimates are free. When you call, you talk to the owner, Vinson Sochirca. You can read more about the owner before you book.

Do I need to be home?

For exterior-only work, usually not, as long as we can get to the windows, gutters or driveway. For inside window cleaning, someone needs to let us in.
